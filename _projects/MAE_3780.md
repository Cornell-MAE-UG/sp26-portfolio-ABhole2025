## Project Overview
For this robotics competition, our team designed and built an autonomous robot tasked with navigating a field, detecting colors, and collecting cubes. The design featured a raised chassis with integrated side walls to funnel and secure cubes underneath the frame, allowing for high-volume collection and rapid movement across the field.

The robot utilized an **Arduino Uno** paired with **H-bridge motor controllers**, **QTI line sensors**, and **color sensors** to differentiate between team-specific targets and field boundaries.

## Individual Contribution
My primary responsibilities focused on the **mechanical assembly** and **electrical integration** of the robot:

* **Mechanical Fabrication:** I led the physical assembly of the robot body, which included modifying the kit's base frame to accommodate larger, laser-cut acrylic wheels. I also fabricated and installed the side walls and rear lift components to ensure the chassis remained level while creating enough clearance for the cubes.
* **Electrical Wiring:** I was responsible for wiring all electronic components to the Arduino Uno. This involved routing connections for the driving motors, H-bridges, and the sensor suite (QTI and color sensors). I ensured the circuitry was robust enough to handle the vibrations and impacts of the competition environment.
* **Iterative Design:** During testing, I assisted in diagnosing a sensor delay issue. I physically relocated the QTI sensors from the rear to the front of the robot, which significantly improved the robot's reaction time to field boundaries and prevented it from driving out of bounds.

## Reflection and Results
The robot performed successfully during the competition, winning several matches. The mechanical "funnel" design proved effective at capturing cubes, though we discovered that the width of the arms occasionally pushed cubes outside the scoring zone during 180-degree turns. This project strengthened my skills in rapid prototyping, circuit troubleshooting, and collaborative hardware development.
