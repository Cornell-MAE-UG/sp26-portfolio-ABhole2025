## Project Overview
For this robotics competition, our team designed and built an autonomous robot tasked with navigating a field, detecting colors, and collecting cubes. The design featured a raised chassis with integrated side walls to funnel and secure cubes underneath the frame.

The robot was controlled by an **Arduino Uno** paired with **H-bridge motor controllers**. **QTI line sensors**, and **color sensors** to were differentiate between field colours, which were essential for navigation.

## Individual Contribution
My primary responsibilities focused on the **mechanical assembly** and **electrical integration** of the robot:

* **Mechanical Assembly:** I led the physical assembly of the robot body, which included modifying the kit's base frame to accommodate larger, laser-cut acrylic wheels, the side walls, and the back extenstion which served to create space under the frame for the cubes. I also installed the color sensors and maintained the integrity of the overall structure as bolts had a tendency to come loose ofter. Additionally, I installed the color sensors and QTI and modified their positions when needed.

* **Electrical Wiring:** I was responsible for wiring all electronic components to the Arduino Uno. This involved routing connections for the driving motors, H-bridges, and the QTI and color sensors. 

* **Iterative Design:** During testing, I assisted in diagnosing a sensor delay issue. I physically relocated the QTI sensors from the rear to the front of the robot, which significantly improved the robot's reaction time to field boundaries and prevented it from driving out of bounds.

## Reflection and Results
The robot performed successfully during the competition, winning several matches. The mechanical "funnel" design proved effective at capturing cubes, though we discovered that the width of the arms occasionally pushed cubes outside the scoring zone during 180-degree turns. This project strengthened my skills in rapid prototyping, circuit troubleshooting, and collaborative hardware development.
